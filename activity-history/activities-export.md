## Notes for Database Import

- The `is_default` flag identifies the 24 pre-seeded system activities
- User-created activities have `is_default: false`
- All activities require a `user_id` foreign key in the new schema
- The `spoons` field accepts integers 0–5
- The `category` field is a free-text label (not a foreign key)
- `description` is optional (nullable)
